    Class: BiologicalContext

    A catalog record of biological identity, at whatever level of specificity a source knows: a taxon, a strain, isolate, cultivar, accession, line or other named lineage, a described genotype, a named population of one taxon, or a community of several taxa. Information about identity, not the biological material itself: samples point at the most specific context known, and a context can point at a broader one.

    Comments

    • At least one of full_name, symbol or taxon must be populated. The first two carry over the AGR AffectedGenomicModel rule; taxon alone admits catalog rows from sources that know only a classification (an NMDC organism with a species taxid and no strain name).
    • One class instead of Organism + GeneticModel, decided 2026-11-12. The 2026-11-11 squad hackathon read organism as a catalog record and put the measured instance in Sample, which left the earlier two-class split holding two catalogs: a taxon catalog (Organism) and a lineage catalog (GeneticModel). Those merge here, with context_type saying which level a row claims, and broader_context relating levels (a described genotype to its strain, a strain row to a species row where a source keeps one). NMDC's strain_name collapses into a strain-type row; AGR's AGM maps to the strain/genotype/line rows; the Tier-1 Option 2 BiologicalClassification maps to the class as a whole.
    • Strain versus genotype, as users mean them. A strain / line / cultivar / accession is a material lineage you can obtain and propagate (it has a stock center, a holding institute, parents). A genotype is a description of genetic composition (alleles, zygosity, background). A strain has a genotype; a genotype can be written down for an individual that belongs to no named lineage. Both NMDC and AGR collapsed the two in practice, and no current BER source records allele-level composition, so one class with context_type carries both, and decomposition stays in has_genomic_components. GENO draws the same line explicitly: GENO:0000536 genotype is information, GENO:0000112 strain is a collection of organisms; an ontologically strict future revision would split along it.
    • Microbes and communities. A pure culture's sample points at the strain or isolate row - no one studies a single microbial cell, so there is no individual-level microbial record to make. A defined mixed culture (SynCom) is a community row whose member_contexts list the strains. An environmental community is a community row whose taxon, when given at all, is a metagenome-style NCBI taxon (soil metagenome, NCBITaxon:410658); its measured composition is data (taxonomic profiles), not catalog metadata, and stays in the source's own tables.
    • Per-kingdom usage of context_type. Plants: cultivar, accession, line (RILs), variety, ecotype (MCPD ranks via MIAPPE infraspecificName). Animals: strain (mouse, rat), genotype (fish, fly). Microbes and fungi: strain, isolate; engineered strains are strains whose has_genomic_components lists the modifications; NCBI Taxonomy sometimes has strain-level taxa (E. coli K-12 MG1655, NCBITaxon:511145), in which case taxon points there and the species rollup comes from the taxonomy, not this schema. Viruses: variant, isolate. Any kingdom: taxon, population, community.
    • What this class is not. Not a Sample: a context is not material, is not taken from a larger body, and is not input to a measurement. The inference directions differ - a sample licenses claims upward about the body it came from; a context licenses generalization downward over its instances, which is the aggregation (knowledge) layer. Not a thing measurements are about: you measure plant 29041, not RIL 407, so feature_of_interest never points here. The sample-shaped neighbors are stocks, seed lots and culture aliquots (TAIR splits Germplasm from Stock for this reason): material instances of a lineage entering a lab workflow are Samples whose biological_context points here.
    • Lakehouse join points. has_genomic_components is the intended JGI connection (gene / transcript / allele CURIEs), anchored by genome_assemblies. The opalplants pennycress registry joins at the line: its germplasm_id (PCG000001...) is a biological_context_id, its passport row fills the material_source_* block, its germplasm_parent rows fill parent_contexts, and its aliases fill synonyms and secondary_identifiers. EMSL MONet connects at study/project level only; nothing context-shaped exists there.
    • Survey of plant resources (2026-11-10; Gramene, TAIR, SorghumBase/GRIN, Ensembl Plants, Phytozome/GOLD, ABRC, pennycress resources): none has a genotype entity. Allelic composition everywhere is an association (TAIR germplasm-to-allele rows carrying zygosity; Ensembl per-variant sample calls), which supports keeping this class name-level and deferring components. Two recurring splits this single class absorbs: (1) the line versus the orderable stock (TAIR Germplasm vs Stock) - the stock side lands in the material_source_* block; (2) the line versus its genome assembly (Ensembl, Phytozome, Gramene) - the assembly side lands in genome_assemblies. GOLD's organism entity has a strain field and no cultivar field, so GOLD organisms map here with context_type strain or cultivar as the source metadata indicates.
