    Class: GeneticModel

    A named genetic entity that organisms instantiate: a strain, genotype, cultivar, accession, line, isolate, ecotype or variant. Covers AGR's AffectedGenomicModel ("inbred strains, stocks, disease models and mutant genotypes") and MIAPPE's BiologicalMaterial (germplasm accessions and named lines). The subtype slot says which kind of entity a record claims to be, because communities draw the strain/genotype line differently rather than because the distinction is crisp.

    Comments

    • At least one of full_name or symbol must be populated (carried over from the AGR AffectedGenomicModel rule).
    • Strain versus genotype, as users mean them. A strain / line / cultivar / accession is a material lineage you can obtain and propagate (it has a stock center, a holding institute, parents). A genotype is a description of genetic composition (alleles, zygosity, background). A strain has a genotype; a genotype can be written down for an individual that belongs to no named lineage. Both NMDC and AGR collapsed the two in practice, and no current BER source records allele-level composition, so this straw person keeps one class with a subtype and leaves decomposition to has_genomic_components.
    • GENO (checked 2026-11-10) makes the same split and is upfront about it: GENO:0000536 genotype is "a specification of the genetic state of an organism" (an information artifact), while GENO:0000112 strain or breed is "a maximal collection of organisms of a single species that have been bred or experimentally manipulated with the goal of being genetically identical" (a collection of material entities; GENO even notes that two colonies with the same genotype maintained in different labs are different strains). So no single GENO term covers this class; the subtype values carry the GENO meanings instead, and an ontologically strict future revision would split GeneticModel along exactly that line. GENO has no term for cultivar or botanical variety; those meanings come from EFO, NCIT and TAXRANK.
    • Per-kingdom usage of subtype. Plants: cultivar, accession, line (RILs), variety, ecotype (MCPD ranks via MIAPPE infraspecificName). Animals: strain (mouse, rat), genotype (fish, fly). Microbes and fungi: strain, isolate; engineered strains are strains whose has_genomic_components lists the modifications; NCBI Taxonomy sometimes has strain-level taxa (e.g. E. coli K-12 MG1655, NCBITaxon:511145), in which case taxon points there and the species-level rollup comes from the taxonomy, not this schema. Viruses: variant, isolate.
    • Lakehouse join points. has_genomic_components is the intended JGI connection (gene / transcript / allele CURIEs). EMSL MONet connects at study/project level only; nothing genotype-shaped exists there.
    • What this class is not. Not an Organism: it is instance-to-type with Organism, parallel to taxon - classification continued below NCBI resolution (its subtypes are MCPD infraspecific ranks), shared by many individuals. The related mapping to nmdc:Organism covers nmdc's type-ish use of that class for microbes, where the strain-bearing record is this entity. Not a Sample, by the umbrella draft's own three conditions: a genotype is not material, and a lineage is not taken from a larger body nor input to a measurement. The inference directions differ - a sample licenses claims upward about the body it came from; a genetic model licenses generalization downward over its instances, which is the aggregation (knowledge) layer, not representativeness. Not a thing measurements are about (the issue #32 MeasuredEntity discussion): you measure plant 29041, not RIL 407, so feature_of_interest never points here. The sample-shaped neighbors are stocks and culture aliquots (TAIR splits Germplasm from Stock for this reason; the umbrella sample_category already has pure_culture and engineered_strain): material instances of a lineage entering a lab workflow are Samples whose identity points here.
    • Survey of plant resources (2026-11-10; Gramene, TAIR, SorghumBase/ GRIN, Ensembl Plants, Phytozome/GOLD, ABRC, pennycress resources): none has a genotype entity. Allelic composition everywhere is an association (TAIR germplasm-to-allele rows carrying zygosity; Ensembl per-variant sample calls), which supports keeping GeneticModel name-level and deferring components. Two recurring splits this single class absorbs, recorded here so mappings stay honest: (1) the line versus the orderable stock (TAIR Germplasm vs Stock, ABRC stock records) - the stock side lands in the material_source_* block; (2) the line versus its genome assembly (Ensembl species databases, Phytozome genome releases, Gramene) - the assembly side lands in genome_assemblies. GOLD's organism entity has a strain field and no cultivar field (cultivars go in strain), so GOLD organisms map to this class with subtype strain or cultivar as the source metadata indicates.
