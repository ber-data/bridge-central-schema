    Class: Organism

    The biological identity of what is in a sample or observation unit: a living or once-living individual (or expected population of one taxon), not the physical sample itself. Several samples can share one organism, and several organisms can instantiate one GeneticModel.

    Comments

    • nmdc:Organism carries strain_name and isolate_name as plain slots. Here that identity moves one hop away: an organism whose strain is known points at a GeneticModel of subtype strain. A source with only a species-level taxon populates taxon and leaves genetic_model empty.
    • In the APPL/MIAPPE plant-phenotyping shape, the ObservationUnit (one pot-grown plant) is the individual; it maps here as an Organism whose genetic_model is the MIAPPE BiologicalMaterial line.
    • This class is instance-level: one individual (a phenotyped plant, a patient) - the Subject of the issue #32 Site/Subject/Sample trio, and one of the things a feature_of_interest may point at (see that slot in the measurements module). Note that nmdc:Organism is used type-ishly (one record of shared biological identity that many OrganismSamples point at via expected_organism), so microbial nmdc:Organism records carrying strain_name map better to GeneticModel than here; the close mapping below covers the individual reading. Whether BRIDGE needs individual-level Organism records for microbial data at all (it probably does not - samples would reference a GeneticModel directly) is a question for the samples squad.

    name: taxon
    description: Taxonomic classification as an NCBITaxon CURIE. On GeneticModel this
      may be a strain-level taxon when NCBI has one; on Organism it is usually species-level.
    comments:
    - Same pattern as nmdc:NcbiTaxon ids and the umbrella draft's taxon slot. Widen
      in lockstep (GTDB, LPSN, SeqCode) if those registries are admitted.
    - Expect asymmetry across kingdoms. Microbial strains often have their own NCBI
      taxid (E. coli K-12 MG1655, NCBITaxon:511145); plant cultivars never do - Ensembl
      Plants gives wheat Jagger the species taxid (4565) and rice MH63 the Indica
      group taxid (39946), and every Phytozome pennycress accession carries the species-level
      13288. On a plant GeneticModel this slot holds the nearest existing taxon, and
      the cultivar identity lives in this class, not in the taxonomy.
